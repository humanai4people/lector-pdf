HumanAI Lector PDF Privacy Policy

HumanAI4People · Effective October 10, 2026

In short: HumanAI Lector PDF does not collect, send or share any personal data. It works 100% on your phone, with no internet, no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

1. What the app does

HumanAI Lector PDF opens PDF files that you choose and reads them aloud using the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone. It also shows the pages and the text so you can read along on screen.

2. Data the app uses and where it stays

  • PDFs you open: the app keeps a copy only in its private storage on your phone, so you can read them again. No other app or person can see it. You can delete it with «Quitar» (Remove) in the recent documents list; uninstalling the app deletes everything.
  • Your progress, bookmarks and settings (last page, chosen voice, speed, appearance): stored only on your phone.
  • Passwords of protected PDFs: used once to open the document and never stored.

3. Data the app does NOT collect

The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send information to any server. It does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, device identifiers or usage statistics. It has no user accounts, no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking tools. Android automatic backup is disabled for this app.

4. Voice

Reading aloud is produced by the phone’s text-to-speech engine (for example, “Speech Services by Google”), using only installed voices that work offline. That engine is part of the Android system and has its own privacy policy.

5. Permissions

  • Notifications: to show the reading controls (play, pause, next).
  • Media playback foreground service: to keep reading with the screen off or while you use another app.
  • Keep the phone awake: only while reading, so it is not interrupted.

The app does not ask for access to all your files: Android only gives it access to the PDF you choose.

6. Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with its new effective date.
